STE Program — Applicant Declaration Form

I. Grade Requirements

I hereby acknowledge that I am aware of and meet the following minimum grade requirements for admission into the Science and Technology Education (STE) Program of Carranglan National High School:

  • At least 85% in English, Science, and Mathematics for the current school year.
  • At least 83% in all other learning areas.
  • No grade lower than 80% in any subject.

  • I understand that admission into the STE Program is conditional upon verification of all submitted requirements.
